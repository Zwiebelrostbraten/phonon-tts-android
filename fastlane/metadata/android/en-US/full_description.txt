Phonon TTS Community is a community-maintained Android system text-to-speech engine based on Gradium Phonon. Use it with reading and accessibility apps that support Android system TTS. Speech runs locally on your CPU after you download a model.

• Seven model choices for English, German, French, Spanish and Portuguese
• Five interface languages, light and dark themes
• 102 downloadable public voice embeddings
• Per-language model selection, speech previews and adjustable speed
• Individual model and voice deletion
• No model weights or voice embeddings bundled in the APK

Optional non-free assets: the six 40M checkpoints are licensed CC-BY-NC-4.0, for noncommercial use, and require Hugging Face approval and a read token. The English phonon-31m-en checkpoint and public voices are Apache-2.0 and do not require an account. Downloaded models have separate licenses from the app code (MIT OR Apache-2.0). Review the model terms before downloading.

The app downloads models only on request and stores your token using Android Keystore encryption. Speech text and generated audio stay on your device. Network access is needed for downloads; inference works offline.

Requires Android 7 or later and ARM64 with dotprod/FP16 support, or x86_64. This independent community fork has a distinct name, icon and application ID; it is not an official Gradium application. It does not migrate data from older installations.
